$$100a + 10b = m^2 \quad \text{ i } \quad c = n^2 - 10b$$

Simplifiquem la segona equació:

$$100a + 10b = m^2 \quad \text{ i } \quad 100a = m^2 - 10b + 10b$$

$$100a = m^2$$
