P(1) + P(2) + P(3) + P(4) + P(5) + P(6) = 1

P(1) + 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 + 1/6 + 2*P(1) = 1

3*P(1) = 2/6
3*P(1) = 1/3
P(1) = 1/9
