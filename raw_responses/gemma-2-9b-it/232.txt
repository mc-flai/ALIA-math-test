    * La suma de totes les parts de l'entrenament és igual a la durada total: x + 20 + (x + 12) = total

    * 2x + 32 = total

    * x = (1/3) * total

    * 2 * ((1/3) * total) + 32 = total

    * (2/3) * total + 32 = total
