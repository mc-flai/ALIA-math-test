The number of permutations of \( n \) distinct items where a specific item \( P \) comes before another specific item \( Q \) is given by half of the total number of permutations. This is because for each permutation where \( P \) is before \( Q \), there is a corresponding permutation where \( Q \) is before \( P \).

Thus, if the total number of permutations is \( n! \), then the number of permutations where \( P \) comes before \( Q \) is:

\[
\boxed{\dfrac{n!}{2}}
