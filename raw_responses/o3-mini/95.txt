Raonament: 
Tenim f(x) = a·x^(2015) + b·x – 1010 i se'ns dóna f(–5) = 5. Substituïm x = –5:
  f(–5) = a·(–5)^(2015) + b·(–5) – 1010.
Com que 2015 és senar, (–5)^(2015) = –5^(2015), així que:
  f(–5) = –a·5^(2015) – 5b – 1010 = 5.
Reordem:
  –a·5^(2015) – 5b = 5 + 1010 = 1015,
o, multiplicant per –1:
  a·5^(2015) + 5b = –1015.
Ara, per calcular f(5):
  f(5) = a·5^(2015) + 5b – 1010.
Per la relació trobada, a·5^(2015) + 5b = –1015, per tant:
  f(5) = –1015 – 1010 = –2025.
