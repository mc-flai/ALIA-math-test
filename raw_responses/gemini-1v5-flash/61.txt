Raonament:

Tenim que $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{9}{4}$ i $\frac{b}{c} = \frac{5}{3}$.  Volem trobar $\frac{a-b}{b-c}$.

De $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{9}{4}$, podem dir que $a = \frac{9}{4}b$.

De $\frac{b}{c} = \frac{5}{3}$, podem dir que $c = \frac{3}{5}b$.

Substituint aquests valors a $\frac{a-b}{b-c}$, obtenim:

$\frac{a-b}{b-c} = \frac{\frac{9}{4}b - b}{b - \frac{3}{5}b} = \frac{\frac{5}{4}b}{\frac{2}{5}b} = \frac{5}{4} \cdot \frac{5}{2} = \frac{25}{8}$

Resposta: B)
